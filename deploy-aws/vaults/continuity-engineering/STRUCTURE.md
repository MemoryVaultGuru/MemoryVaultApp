# Structure: Continuity Engineering

1. **Objectives**: What each service promises to survive, agreed with whoever owns it. One objective per page, with the unit it is stated in and the review that exercised it. (2 notes, has TEMPLATE.md)
2. **Runbooks**: One procedure per page, written for somebody who has not read it before. Every step that needs judgement links the page with the criterion. (1 note, has TEMPLATE.md)
3. **Reviews**: What an exercise actually measured. It is the only place a figure may be asserted, and it is what turns an objective from a target into a capability. (1 note, has TEMPLATE.md)
